About SIMAT

Sreepathy Institute of Management and Technology (SIMAT) is a premier engineering institution committed to nurturing technical excellence and innovation. Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University and approved by AICTE, we provide a dynamic learning environment that bridges the gap between academic knowledge and industry requirements.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in engineering and technology, creating innovative and socially responsible professionals.

Our Mission

To provide quality technical education, foster research and innovation, and instil ethical values for sustainable development.
